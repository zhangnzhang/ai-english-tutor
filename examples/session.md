# Example session (fictional learner)

The learner here is invented: Mei, a nurse in Chengdu who has a cat and runs on weekends. Her error log already contains *dose* for *does*, *doesn't has*, and *did went*.

## 1. The tutor opens from the files

> Welcome back. Last time you passed the present simple quiz 8/10. Two items are due for review today: G1 (spelling of *does*) and G4 (base verb after *did*). Three quick questions, then a mixed set on present and past simple questions and negatives.

## 2. A practice set

Half multiple choice, half fill-in. The wrong options come from Mei's own error log, the sentences are about her life, and each has a line in her language.

> **Choose (answer with digits, e.g. 3 1 4 2)**
>
> 1. ___ your cat sleep on your bed?
>    1) Do  2) Dose  3) Is  4) Does
>    （你的猫睡在你床上吗？）
> 2. I ___ to work yesterday because it rained.
>    1) didn't ran  2) didn't run  3) doesn't ran  4) not run
>    （昨天下雨，所以我没有跑步去上班。）
> 3. My colleague Lan ___ a night shift every Friday.
>    1) has  2) have  3) is have  4) does has
>    （我的同事兰每周五都上夜班。）
> 4. Where ___ you go last weekend?
>    1) do  2) was  3) did  4) were
>    （你上周末去了哪儿？）
>
> **Fill in (write everything that goes in the blank)**
>
> 5. My manager ___ (not / like) noisy wards these days.（我的主管现在不喜欢吵闹的病房。）
> 6. ___ Lan ___ (work) in the same hospital last year?（兰去年在同一家医院工作吗？）
> 7. I ___ (see) a good movie last night.（我昨晚看了一部好电影。）
> 8. The environment in our hospital ___ (be) very clean now.（我们医院的环境现在非常干净。）

Answer positions in this set: 4, 2, 1, 3. *shift*, *ward*, *colleague* and *environment* are on her vocabulary list.

## 3. Grading

Mei answers `2 2 4 3`, then `doesn't likes`, `Did / worked`, `saw`, `is`.

> **4 / 8**
>
> 1. ❌ Dose your cat sleep → **Does** your cat sleep on your bed?
>    Spelling: d-o-e-s. *dose* is a different word (an amount of medicine). (G1)
> 2. ✅ I didn't run to work yesterday because it rained.
> 3. ❌ Lan does has → My colleague Lan **has** a night shift every Friday.
>    A plain positive statement needs no helper *does*. The helper belongs in questions and negatives, and after it the verb is *have*, not *has*. (G2)
> 4. ✅ Where did you go last weekend?
> 5. ❌ doesn't likes → My manager **doesn't like** noisy wards these days.
>    After *doesn't* the verb goes back to its base form; the *-s* already lives in *does*. (G2)
> 6. ❌ Did Lan worked → Did Lan **work** in the same hospital last year?
>    Same idea in the past: *did* carries the past, the verb stays plain. (G4)
> 7. ✅ I saw a good movie last night.
> 8. ✅ The environment in our hospital is very clean now.
>
> The pattern in 3, 5 and 6 is one idea: when a helper (*does*, *did*) is in the sentence, it carries the ending and the main verb stays bare. Next set is only that.
>
